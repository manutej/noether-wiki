## Why this corpus

These problems are how a trading firm trains the same muscles as real computer science: invariants, search, measure, and glue. They are not leetcode. They are not "brain teasers" in the party sense. A working programmer who wants the mathematics back should sit with one of them the way they would sit with a hard invariant in a concurrent system.

## Series in this window

- [Hooks](series/hooks.md) — L-partition of a square, uniqueness constraints, pentomino overlay.
- [Knight Moves](series/knight-moves.md) — knight graph, scoring along a path, reconstruction.
- [Robot](series/robot.md) — games against nature or an opponent; Nash, thresholds, φ.
- [Number Cross](series/number-cross.md) — region constraints + concatenation arithmetic.
- [Subtiles / tiling](series/tiling.md) — finite geometry that wants a global section.
