# Number Cross

A crossword whose "words" are integers satisfying row/column predicates, usually with regions that share a digit and with black cells acting as blocks.

In this window:

- [Number Cross 5](../puzzles/2025-05-number-cross-5.md) — May 2025.

CS bridge: constraint programming. The concatenation constraint is a channeled view of the same cells as both digits and integers. That channel is the restriction map.
