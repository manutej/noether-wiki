# Tiling / Subtiles / Fences

Finite geometry problems whose answer is usually an enumeration or a product extracted from a completed tiling that also satisfies a global symmetry.

In this window:

- [Fences 2](../puzzles/2024-09-fences-2.md) — September 2024. Hex grid, posts, a 120-degree loop through six colors.
- [Some Ones, Somewhere](../puzzles/2025-06-some-ones-somewhere.md) — June 2025. Partridge tilings; the ones give coordinates.
- [Subtiles 2](../puzzles/2026-02-subtiles-2.md) — February 2026.
- [Arch Madness](../puzzles/2026-05-arch-madness.md) — May 2026. Quarter-circle arcs as region boundaries.

CS bridge: a tiling is a global section of a sheaf of local matching rules. The product-over-regions answer format is a way of hashing that section.
